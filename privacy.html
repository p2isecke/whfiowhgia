Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

The short version: WHFIOWHGIA doesn't collect your personal information. There are no accounts, forms, ads, analytics or tracking cookies.

A few settings (like your theme and Spoiler-free mode) are saved in your own browser, and you can clear them anytime.

Who runs this site

WHFIOWHGIA ("Will He Finish It Or Will He Give It Away?") is an unofficial fan site about Deep Pocket Monster's complete-the-set challenges. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Deep Pocket Monster, Card Party, The Pokémon Company, Nintendo, Creatures or GAME FREAK. In this policy, "we" means the person who runs this fan site.

Information we collect

None. The site has no sign-up, no contact forms, no comments, no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools. We don't sell, share or rent any information about you, because we don't have any.

Information saved on your device

The site uses your browser's local storage to remember a few choices so they're still set next time you visit:

  • pvs-theme: whether you picked auto, light or dark mode.
  • pvs-spoiler-free: whether Spoiler-free mode is turned on.
  • pvs-seen: whether you've already revealed the result of the latest challenge.

These stay in your browser and are never sent to us or anyone else. They aren't cookies and aren't used to track you. To remove them, clear this site's data in your browser settings. The site works normally without them.

Services the site relies on

Like nearly every website, loading this page involves a few outside services. They may receive basic technical information that every browser sends, such as your IP address, browser type and the page you requested.

  • Website hosting. The company that hosts the site may keep standard server logs for security and reliability, under its own privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts. The site's typefaces load from Google Fonts. See Google's Privacy Policy and the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.
  • YouTube. Videos are not embedded. Nothing is sent to YouTube unless you click a "Watch" link, at which point you're on YouTube and its privacy policy applies.

Set logos and card images are stored on this site itself, not loaded from other websites.

Children's privacy

Lots of Pokémon fans are kids, and that's part of why this site collects nothing. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Your rights

Because we don't hold personal information about you, there's nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Data handled by the hosting provider, Google Fonts or YouTube is covered by their own policies, and you can make requests to them directly.

Changes to this policy

If the site ever adds something that collects information, this page will be updated before that happens, with a new effective date at the top.
