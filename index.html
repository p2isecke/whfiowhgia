About the data

Covers the complete-set challenges only, from Sep 2022 to Oct 2026. Side quests are listed separately and don't count toward the score.

For attempts #1 to #20, results come from the running record DPM states in each video's description (for example, "my current record is 10 wins and 9 losses"). For #21 to #27 he stopped stating it, so those results come from the video comments, where fans congratulate or commiserate and some keep a tally. Their tallies match this scoreboard.
